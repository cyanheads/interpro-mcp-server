## [0.1.1](changelog/0.1.x/0.1.1.md) — 2026-10-21

First public release: six read-only tools for InterPro entries, protein domain maps, and clans over the keyless EMBL-EBI InterPro API, with 100 KB response budgets that continue by whole record, on STDIO or Streamable HTTP.
